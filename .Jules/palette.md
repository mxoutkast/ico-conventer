## 2025-02-18 - Keyboard Shortcuts for List Interactions
**Learning:** Users expect standard keyboard shortcuts (Delete, Ctrl+A) in list views, but they are often missing in Tkinter apps by default. Visual hints on buttons (e.g., "Remove (Del)") improve discoverability.
**Action:** Always bind <Delete> and <Control-a> to Treeview widgets and update button labels to hint at shortcuts.
